skyler stein

Skyler Stein brings people’s ideas to life. It started in experiential design, where the work is deciding how a moment feels before anyone arrives: who’s in the room, where they sit, what happens first. Over a decade as a creative strategist, consultant, and creative director, including through her agency Holyduck, she has built brands, campaigns, and stories from that same instinct. That through line is how she became the founder of Skydeas, a platform where creators protect, build, and sell their ideas. Underneath it all is a love of community: bringing bright minds together and making room for real conversation. She values human connection, and her passion has always been helping people create, so building a table for them is a natural path.

their

Lucy builds the plate. Skyler builds the room. Two founders who lifted each other through their own process, now bringing that same feeling to a table of people. That’s the X Table.

lucy mello

Lucy Mello is a private chef based in Miami, redefining the basics of cooking. Self-taught by choice, she cooks without a rulebook and through her own lens, drawing on her Brazilian roots, a foundation in French technique, and a constant pull to push boundaries. Her food is designed to make you pause, look twice, and reconsider what you assumed was on the plate. At the heart of it is a simple belief: meaningful connections are built around a table. The food is just the excuse.
